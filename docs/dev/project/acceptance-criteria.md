| The carrier detect's squelch follows the band **behind any receive filter** (#1452, REQ-DCD-01 restated). The floor was a 25th percentile ACROSS the 300–2700 Hz bins scaled as if the noise were white; behind a 500 Hz or 250 Hz filter most bins are stopband, the floor collapsed to the 0.001 clamp, every block read as carrier, and BPSK250 frames the decoder could read (5/8 from their offset in the slab, 8/8 alone) decoded **0/8** through the daemon's path. It is now each bin's noise level over time (a trimmed mean — a quantile correction alone inflated a steady tone 3.4×), summed: the power the block RMS sees. It is **held while a burst is gathered** (committed on a cap flush, or for a burst too short to hold a preamble when the hold IS that burst; discarded otherwise), and each block is **judged against the floor from before it** — learning first let a read holding a whole frame teach the floor that frame (found by the twin rig). The operator squelch is a LOWER bound (it was silently overwritten every window), default 0; the clamp is 1e-4. A failed OTA burst shorter than every candidate's recognition window (acquisition window plus one symbol; NOT `min_frame_samples`, which on MFSK16/SL1 is the whole 17 s frame) is not ladder evidence — judged by DURATION, never by how many reads delivered it (a first build that dropped any one-read burst also dropped a 16 000-sample failure arriving in one read). That makes idle flicker rarer, not non-evidence: longer flickers count, and the NACK streak has no time decay (#1456). The cross-band repeater does not transmit on a cold floor: a sense that started before rig_b's floor was warm (16 windows) is deferred, not trusted — the #1325 gate caught it keying onto a busy band — and each session primes the floor first; a band occupied for most of the first cold read is still learned as the floor (#1454). A hold no burst owns is released — without that, the floor froze after a step up and stayed at 2.49× the idle once the band dropped back (measured; now 1.24×). **Not fixed here:** after the band steps UP the squelch recovers only at the cap flush (measured at BPSK250: 37 s; by the cap rule ~5 min under `hpx_hf`'s entry candidates — #1455), and BPSK31 on a wide filter was still not gathered at +12 dB in-band — closed by #1454 stage 2, next row | `cargo test -p openpulse-modem --no-default-features --test dcd_floor_follows_the_filter` + `cargo test -p openpulse-dsp --no-default-features --lib noise_floor` + `cargo test -p openpulse-daemon --no-default-features --test twin_daemon_bridge` + `cargo test -p openpulse-repeater --no-default-features --test carrier_sense` |
